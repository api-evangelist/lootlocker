Games have been shaped by their communities since digital distribution made it possible. Early Access, modding, crowdfunding, open betas: the players who show up early are the ones who tell you what works and what doesn't, and they are usually the same people who become evangelists and tell everyone else about your game once it's launched (especially if you give them something exclusive for being an early supporter). Playtesting sits right at the front of that.
